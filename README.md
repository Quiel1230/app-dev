# My Favorite Movie: The Amazing Spider-Man 2

**The Amazing Spider-Man 2** (2014)
is a superhero sequel starring *Andrew Garfield* as Peter Parker, who balances his life as a high school graduate and Spider-Man while facing powerful new threats from Oscorp.

## Why I Like It
- Amazing visual effects and high-energy web-swinging scenes
- Intense battles against Electro and the Green Goblin
- Emotional storyline between Peter Parker and Gwen Stacy

## Top 3 Favorite Characters
1. Peter Parker / Spider-Man
2. Gwen Stacy
3. Max Dillon / Electro

> "Every day I wake up knowing that the more people I try to save, the more enemies I will make." — Peter Parker
